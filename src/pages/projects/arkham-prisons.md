## Arkham Prisons

**Arkham Studios** was a premier Minecraft: Bedrock Edition server studio, best known for its feature-rich gamemodes and creative server experiences. Among its standout offerings was **Arkham Prisons**, a dynamic and competitive mining-based gamemode that captivated a large player base during its run.

## My Role

As a **Server Developer**, I was deeply involved in the development of **Arkham Prisons**, contributing to both its technical foundation and core gameplay. My responsibilities included:

- 🔧 **Server Development** — Built and maintained scalable server-side systems optimized for Minecraft: Bedrock Edition.
- 🛠 **Gameplay Feature Implementation** — Designed and integrated major gameplay mechanics including **custom enchantments**, **progressive mining systems**, and **in-game economies**.
- 🎨 **Collaborative Design** — Worked closely with game designers to bring concepts to life through code, ensuring a balanced and engaging experience.
- ⚙️ **Performance Optimization** — Integrated and fine-tuned plugins for a seamless experience, even under high player concurrency.
- 🚀 **Launch & Live Ops** — Supported launch efforts and handled post-launch stability, updates, and player feedback loops.

## Impact

- 📈 Contributed to a highly polished *Prisons* experience that drew thousands of concurrent players at its peak.
- 🧱 Built a flexible codebase, enabling future expansion and easier maintenance.
- 🎉 Played a key role in the successful launch and operations of one of Arkham Studios’ flagship gamemodes.
