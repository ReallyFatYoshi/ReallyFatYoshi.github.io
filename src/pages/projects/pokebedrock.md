## The Project

**PokéBedrock** is a fast-growing Minecraft: Bedrock Edition server with a community of over 32,000 players and a global development team. The platform delivers unique and engaging gameplay experiences through custom-built systems and ongoing development under the PokéBedrock brand.

## My Role

As a **Developer**, I played a major role in the ongoing development of the server's backend, gameplay systems, and infrastructure. My work included:

- 🧩 **Plugin Development** — Created and maintained custom plugins for the **PocketMine-MP** server software, enabling gameplay features and mechanics tailored to our community.
- 🖥 **Infrastructure Engineering** — Helped design and build scalable, resilient backend infrastructure to support high concurrent player counts and cross-server features.
- 🔐 **Security Enhancements** — Contributed to securing both server and client interactions, patching exploits and hardening critical systems.
- ⚙️ **Addon Development Support** — Assisted in the development and integration of Minecraft Bedrock add-ons for extended game functionality.

## Impact

- 🎮 Enabled stable gameplay for tens of thousands of players with consistently high retention
- 🔄 Introduced technical solutions that improved maintainability and performance
- 🤝 Strengthened the server's ecosystem through plugin extensibility and team collaboration
